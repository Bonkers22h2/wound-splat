# Dataset & Software Citations

Every dataset here is used for **nonprofit academic research only** and none of the raw
data is redistributed. Each entry records the terms it is used under, because the terms
differ between them.

## Datasets

The two UWM datasets below are used under a data-use permission granted by
Prof. Zeyun Yu (UWM Big Data Lab, 2026-09-04) for nonprofit academic research.

### DFUTissue (wound tissue-type segmentation)
> M. K. Dhar, C. Wang, Y. Patel, T. Zhang, J. Niezgoda, S. Gopalakrishnan, K. Chen, Z. Yu,
> "Wound Tissue Segmentation in Diabetic Foot Ulcer Images Using Deep Learning: A Pilot Study,"
> arXiv:2406.16012, 2024.

Repository: https://github.com/uwm-bigdata/DFUTissueSegNet

### AZH Wound Care Center patches (wound vs. background segmentation)
> C. Wang, D. M. Anisuzzaman, V. Williamson, M. K. Dhar, B. Rostami, J. Niezgoda,
> S. Gopalakrishnan, Z. Yu, "Fully Automatic Wound Segmentation with Deep Convolutional
> Neural Networks," Scientific Reports, 10:21897, 2020.

Repository: https://github.com/uwm-bigdata/wound-segmentation

### WoundTissue (necrosis, slough, tendon and bone segmentation)

**Licence: Creative Commons Attribution-NonCommercial-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0).**
Free for non-commercial research with attribution. No permission request is required, so
this dataset is **not** covered by the UWM permission above.

> M. A. Kabir, N. Roy, M. E. Hossain, J. Featherston, S. Ahmed, "Deep Learning for Wound
> Tissue Segmentation: A Comprehensive Evaluation using A Novel Dataset," arXiv:2502.10652,
> 2025.

Repository: https://github.com/akabircs/WoundTissue

Terms as they apply here:

- **Attribution** — the citation above must accompany any reported result.
- **NonCommercial** — matches the existing 3DGS licence constraint on this project.
- **ShareAlike** applies to the dataset and to adaptations of the dataset, not to this
  repository's code. Raw images stay out of version control. No derived masks or trained
  weights are redistributed without re-checking these terms first.
